Python & OpenCV

Biomimetic Ocean-Cleaning Robot

[ Detailed Robot Diagram/Code Snippet ]

For this project, I explored how technology can solve real-world environmental issues (SDG 14: Life Below Water). I designed a cardboard prototype inspired by the movement of manta rays.

Instead of just building the physical structure, I integrated computer vision using Python and YOLOv8 to allow the system to visually track objects. This project taught me how to bridge the gap between mechanical engineering and artificial intelligence.

Logic Pro & Arrangement

Music Theory & Composition

[ Screenshot of Logic Pro Timeline ]

Beyond STEM, music is my creative outlet. In this project, I focused on analyzing complex chord progressions (like 6-4-5-1 structures) and melody arrangements in R&B and Pop music.

Using Logic Pro and MIDI instruments, I experimented with producing my own tracks and writing original lyrics in both Chinese and English. It’s a process that balances technical precision with emotional storytelling.
